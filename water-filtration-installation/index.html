Water Tastes Like Chlorine, Looks Cloudy or Leaves Stains? A Filter Can Fix That

Tap water that tastes like a pool, runs cloudy after a main repair or leaves orange stains in the sink is telling you something about what is in it. The fix is usually a filter chosen for the problem. A system that treats sediment is different from one that treats chlorine or lead, which is why water filtration installation starts with a question about what you see and taste.

Overview

How Water Filtration Installation Works in Frederick

Water filtration installation in Frederick starts with a plan. City water here starts as river and reservoir water, treated by the city and partly topped up with Potomac supply from the county. The city's published consumer report lists what is in it, and a home on a private well needs its own test.

A whole-house system treats every tap, protects fixtures and appliances and suits sediment, chlorine and iron. An under-sink or countertop unit treats drinking and cooking water only and suits lead or taste concerns. Many homes use both, with a sediment filter at the main and a certified unit at the kitchen sink.

Maryland restricts who may install treatment equipment. Water conditioning and treatment work must be done by a licensed well driller, water conditioner installer, master plumber or journeyman plumber. That is also why the install needs a bypass valve, a shutoff and a clear space for filter changes.

Lead and the City's Survey

Service Line Records and What a Certified Filter Is For

The city has put out a searchable list of service line materials by address, sorting each line into non-lead, lead, galvanized needing replacement, or unknown. Look up your address. If your line is listed as lead or unknown, replacing it is the lasting answer, and a certified filter reduces exposure in the meantime.

A lead-reducing filter must be certified to NSF/ANSI 53 for lead, not just labeled as a carbon filter. Change cartridges on the maker's schedule. A spent cartridge stops working without warning. For lines that need replacing, see emergency water line repair and replacement.

A filter is only as good as its maintenance. Put cartridge dates on a calendar. Many systems include an indicator or a flow-based reminder.

Local Notes

Water Filtration for Frederick Homes

City water in Frederick is treated and tested, so filtration there is mostly about taste and chlorine. A carbon filter under the sink or at the main covers it.

Wells outside the city, including those near Thurmont and the Catoctin foothills, may need sediment and iron filtration. A lab test tells you which stage to add.
