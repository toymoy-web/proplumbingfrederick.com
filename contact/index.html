How to Contact the Emergency Line

Call (301) 936-0389 for any plumbing problem. Active leaks, backups, no hot water and other urgent problems are answered at any hour. Planned work such as a water heater replacement or a repipe estimate can be requested on the same number during any part of the day.

There is no contact form on this site, by design. A phone call gets a person who can ask about the problem and tell you what to shut off. For a non-urgent question, email contact@proplumbingfrederick.com and expect a reply in business hours.

If you smell gas, see sparks or smoke, or someone is hurt, call 911 first.
