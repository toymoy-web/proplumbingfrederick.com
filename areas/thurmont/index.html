    Winter First

    Emergency Plumber in Thurmont, MD 21788: Freezing Weather Drives the Calls

    Emergency plumber calls in Thurmont peak when the thermometer drops. The town sits at the foot of Catoctin Mountain, and nights in the foothills can run colder than in Frederick. Homes with pipes in exterior walls, garages and crawlspaces split first, and the damage often appears days later when the pipe thaws and begins to spray.

    The county's building code sets the frost line at 30 inches, which protects buried lines but not the pipes inside your walls. If you have a cabin or a second home, keep the heat on and drain the system before leaving it in winter. A pipe that freezes in an empty house can run for days before anyone sees it.

    Cold Weather

    Warning Signs Before a Thurmont Pipe Freezes

    Frost on exposed pipes

    White frost on a garage or crawlspace pipe means it is at or below freezing.

    A faucet that trickles

    Reduced flow on a cold morning can mean ice is forming inside the line.

    Odd noises in the wall

    Knocking or creaking as water pushes past ice means pressure is building.

    Cold exterior rooms

    Pipes behind bathrooms on outside walls freeze first in older homes.

    Hose bibs left connected

    A hose attached in winter traps water that freezes into the pipe wall.

    An unheated vacation property

    Cabins and second homes lose heat when no one is there.

    Spring Water

    Hunting Creek, Owens Creek and the Emergency Basement Water That Follows

    Thurmont sits between the Hunting Creek and Owens Creek watersheds. Big Hunting Creek runs through Cunningham Falls State Park and down into town, and the park's lake and the creeks swell after a heavy rain. Homes near the creeks or at the base of slopes are the first to see water in the basement.

    A sump pump with a battery backup is the standard defense. The pump moves water out, but a pump needs power, and storms cut power. If your pit fills faster than the pump can clear it, have the system checked before spring.

    The town's public water comes from groundwater wells, and the state treats at least one of them as influenced by surface water, so read any notice the town sends after heavy rain. Rural properties outside the town system use private wells, and well pumps fail at inconvenient times. If you lose pressure on a well, check the breaker and pressure tank first.

    Before We Arrive

    Before We Arrive: If You Have a Burst Pipe or Active Leak in Thurmont

    Shut off the main valve

    Close the main shutoff. If the house is on a well, also switch off the pump breaker so it stops pushing water into the leak.

    Kill power to wet areas

    Turn off the water heater and any wet circuit. Never use a torch or open flame to thaw a pipe, use a hair dryer or heating pad.

    Clear the area

    Open the nearest faucet so melting water can drain, photograph the burst section and keep the broken piece for the insurer.

    Call the 24/7 line

    Give the street name and say if the house has been vacant or the heat was off. A burst pipe in a vacant property is urgent.
