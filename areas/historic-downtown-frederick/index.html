The Area

Plumber for Historic Downtown Frederick: Homes, Shops and Restaurants Together

The Frederick Historic District is the city's downtown core. Reference sources describe more than 200 retailers, restaurants and antique shops along Market, Patrick and East Streets, with Carroll Creek Park and a theater district near the Weinberg Center for the Arts. The district appears in the American Planning Association's 2010 list of Great Neighborhoods.

Downtown also has residents. The City's Neighborhood Advisory Council 11 represents downtown residents, and the City's Historic Preservation Commission and the Downtown Frederick Partnership publish guides for residents, contractors and merchants, plus the Frederick Town Historic District Design Guidelines.

Shared Buildings

When Homes and Businesses Share a Building

A leak above a ceiling does not stop at the unit where it starts. In a building with shared walls or floors, water from an upstairs fixture can reach the space below, so the first calls include the building owner or manager as well as a plumber.

Restaurants and food shops have their own needs, such as kitchen drains, hand sinks and water heaters sized for steady use. Tell the dispatcher if the call is for a business so the right crew is planned.

Many downtown buildings are older, but age alone does not decide what is inside them. A plumber should inspect the actual pipes before recommending a repair or a replacement.

First Steps

Before Plumbing Work on a Downtown Building

  1. Know who is responsible

    Check whether the owner, a landlord or the tenant handles the pipe in question.

  2. Stop the water

    Close the fixture valve or the main for your space. Do not force a stuck valve.

  3. Tell the building contact

    Notify the manager or neighbors if water may reach other units.

  4. Ask about exterior changes

    If work will change anything visible from the street, ask the City's Historic Preservation staff whether review applies.

  5. Call the phone line

    Tell the dispatcher your street, whether it is a home or a business and where the water is coming from.
