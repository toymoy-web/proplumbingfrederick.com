Where the Emergency Line Reaches

The city of Frederick is covered across its four ZIP codes, 21701, 21702, 21703 and 21704, from the historic district to the subdivisions on the edges of town. Beyond the city, seven nearby communities have their own pages, because the housing, the water and the weather differ from one to the next. A cold-weather pipe in the foothills, a town well in the valley and a planned community with newer plumbing all call for different questions from the dispatcher.

If your address is not on the list, call anyway. The dispatcher confirms coverage by street, and a call costs nothing.

Towns We Serve

  • Middletown, MD 21769Historic Main Street, valley farms and private wells.
  • Brunswick, MD 21716Potomac railroad town with older pipes and river-valley basements.

  • Thurmont, MD 21788Mountain foothills, creeks and cold-weather pipe calls.
  • Urbana, MD 21704Planned villages built since 1999 and newer plumbing.

Services Available in Every Area

Every town gets the full list of services on the services page, from burst pipe repair work to water heater replacement and drain cleaning. Wells and septic systems are common outside the town centers, so well pump repair and septic help are available too.
