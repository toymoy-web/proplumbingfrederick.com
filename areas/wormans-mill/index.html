Worman's Mill is in the 21701 part of Frederick, a short drive from downtown with access to Route 26, I-70 and I-270. Neighborhood profile sites say the community dates back to 1991 and has kept growing since, with listed homes ranging from 1989 to 2019.

Owner Questions

Plumbing Questions That Fit a Planned Community

In a townhouse or condominium, a leak does not always stay in one home. Water from a supply line or an overflowing fixture can reach a neighbor through a shared wall or floor, so the order of your first calls matters.

Start by shutting off water to the fixture or the home. Then tell your association or building manager, because they may need to shut off a shared line or notify other owners. Take photos before anything is cleaned up.

Water heaters in townhomes and condos are often in a closet, attic or utility room. Ask your association whether it has rules on replacement, venting or scheduling, then plan the work before the old tank gives out.

First Steps

If Water Is Running in Your Townhome or Condo

  1. Stop the water

    Close the fixture valve or your home's main shutoff. If it will not turn, do not force it.

  2. Protect electrical areas

    Switch off breakers for rooms with water near outlets or lights.

  3. Tell the association

    Call the management contact so shared lines can be shut off and neighbors warned.

  4. Photograph the damage

    Take clear photos of the source and any wet walls or ceilings for your insurer.

  5. Call the phone line

    Give the dispatcher your street, your unit type and where the water is coming from.
