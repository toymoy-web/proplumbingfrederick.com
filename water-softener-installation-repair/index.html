Hard Water

Signs the Water in Your Home Is Too Hard

White crust on faucets and showerheads

Calcium and magnesium dry into scale.

Spotty glasses and dishes

Minerals leave a film even after the dishwasher runs.

Stiff laundry and dry skin

Soap does not lather well in hard water.

Water heater noise and short life

Scale on the bottom of the tank insulates the burner.

Low flow from old fixtures

Scale narrows pipes and aerators.

Orange or brown staining

On wells, iron adds rust-colored stains.

Overview

How Water Softener Installation and Repair Works in Frederick

Water softener installation in Frederick begins with a number. A test measures hardness in grains per gallon or mg/L, and on a well it also checks iron, pH and sediment. City of Frederick water averages about 100 mg/L, or six grains per gallon, which is only slightly hard. Many homeowners find that still leaves scale, and a softener is a reasonable choice.

A softener works by passing water through a tank of resin beads that trade sodium or potassium for the calcium and magnesium. When the beads fill, the unit flushes them with brine from a salt tank. Sizing depends on household size and hardness, and an undersized unit runs out between regenerations.

Plumbing connections to the unit follow the International Plumbing Code (IPC) that Maryland adopts, including an air gap on the drain line. Maryland law says that installing or modifying water conditioning equipment can be done only by a licensed well driller, a licensed water conditioner installer, a master plumber or a journeyman plumber. Ask who is licensed for the job, and who pulls the permit for the drain and electrical connections.

Repair or Replace

When a Softener Gets a Repair and When It Needs Replacing

A repair is reasonable when

  • The control valve or a seal has failed.
  • The brine line is clogged.
  • The unit is under 12 years old.
  • Resin is still in good condition.

Replacement is the better choice when

  • The tank is cracked or leaking.
  • The resin is exhausted after 15 or more years.
  • Repair costs approach the price of a new unit.
  • Hardness has changed and the old unit is undersized.

Resin beads break down over time. Water that tests hard right after regeneration means the resin or the valve is failing.

Warranty and Salt

Softener warranties often cover the tank for years and the control valve for a shorter time. Keep the receipt and the model number. Use salt of the grade the maker recommends.

Local Notes

Water Softeners in Frederick and the Surrounding Wells
