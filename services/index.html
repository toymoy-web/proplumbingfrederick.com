All Services

Plumbing Services We Cover

  • Water Heater Installation & ReplacementTank, tankless and heat pump units sized and installed.
  • Water Damage RestorationDrying, cleanup and rebuilding after a burst pipe, sewer backup or flood.
  • Drain Cleaning & UncloggingClogged sinks, tubs, toilets and main lines cleared with cable and camera.
  • Water Heater RepairNo hot water, leaks and pilot trouble diagnosed before parts are ordered.
  • Boiler RepairHot water and steam boilers: no heat, leaks and pressure loss.
  • Leak DetectionMeter tests and listening tools that find hidden leaks.
  • Water MitigationFirst-day extraction, protection and drying setup after a water loss.
  • Sewer Line RepairCamera inspection, spot repair, lining and replacement of the sewer lateral.
  • Sewer Backup RepairEmergency clearing and cause tracing when sewage comes up a drain.
  • Hydro JettingHigh-pressure water cleaning for grease, scale and roots.
  • Burst Pipe RepairFrozen, corroded and surge-damaged pipes repaired and tested.
  • Tankless Water Heater ServiceDescaling, error codes and repairs for tankless units.
  • Sump Pump Installation & RepairPump repair, replacement and battery backup for wet basements.
  • Basement WaterproofingThe drain and pump side of a wet basement, with cheaper fixes explained first.
  • Repiping & Whole-Home Pipe ReplacementWhole-home supply pipe replacement for galvanized, failing copper and polybutylene.
  • Water Line Repair & ReplacementRepair or replacement of the buried pipe from the meter to the house.
  • Toilet Repair & InstallationRunning, leaking and clogged toilets repaired, and new toilets installed.
  • Faucet & Sink RepairDrips, leaks, traps and supply lines at kitchen and bath sinks.
  • Garbage Disposal Repair & InstallationJams, leaks and new disposal installs with dishwasher hookups.
  • Gas Line Installation & RepairGas piping for ranges, dryers, heaters and grills, pressure tested.
  • Water Softener Installation & RepairHard water treatment for city water and private wells.
  • Water Filtration InstallationWhole-house and under-sink filters matched to your water.
  • Backflow Testing & PreventionBackflow device testing, repair and report filing.
  • Shower & Bathtub InstallationThe plumbing side of a new shower or tub: valves, drains and fixtures.
  • Commercial PlumbingRestaurants, shops, offices and apartment buildings.
  • Septic Tank PumpingSeptic backups, house lines and where a licensed hauler fits in.
  • Well Pump RepairNo water, low pressure and cycling pumps on private wells.
