Summary

What This Site Collects

This website does not have contact forms, user accounts or a store, so it does not ask you to type personal information into a page. Like most sites, the server and the hosting provider may log technical details when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you view and the time. These logs are used to keep the site secure and working.

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when the font files load. Embedded maps are provided by Google, and loading a map may share data with Google under its own policies.

Calls

What Happens When You Call

The phone numbers on this site are tracking numbers that route calls to a participating plumbing professional. The call routing and tracking system may record the caller's phone number, the time and the length of the call, and calls may be recorded or monitored for quality and tracking. If a call is recorded, you should hear a notice at the start of the call.

Information you give on a call, such as your name, address and the problem, is used to arrange service. It is shared with the contractor who handles your request.

Your Choices

Sharing, Retention and Your Rights

This site does not state that it sells personal information. Information is shared with service providers and with the contractor assigned to your call, and where required by law. Call records are kept as long as needed for service, billing and legal purposes.

You can ask what information is held about you, or ask for its deletion where the law allows, by emailing contact@proplumbingfrederick.com. This policy may change, and the date of the last update is the build date of this site, 2026-10-03.

To reach the business, see the contact page.
